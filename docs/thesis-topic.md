# Thesis Topic

**Polish (original):**

> Projekt i implementacja systemu informatycznego zwiększającego bezpieczeństwo transakcji biznesowych,
> przez wykorzystanie smart kontraktów Blockchain

**English translation:**

> Design and Implementation of an Information System Increasing the Security of Business Transactions
> Through the Use of Blockchain Smart Contracts

## Objective

The goal of the thesis is to design and implement an information system that increases
the security of transactions between large enterprises through the use of smart contracts
known from Blockchain technology. The choice of implementation technologies is at the
discretion of the graduate student.
